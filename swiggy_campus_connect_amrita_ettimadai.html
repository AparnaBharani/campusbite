Phase 2 • Scheduled External Logistics

Outside Restaurant Bulk-Delivery to Main Gate

External restaurant delivery is intentionally excluded from our Phase 1 MVP to keep campus entry controlled and unit economics sharp. Once internal canteen order density is validated at Amrita Ettimadai, Phase 2 unlocks scheduled batch deliveries from Coimbatore city restaurants.

01

Scheduled Batch Windows

Students place outside orders before fixed cutoff slots (e.g., 12:00 PM lunch batch, 7:00 PM dinner batch) to pool delivery costs down to ₹15–₹25 per student.

02

Restaurant-Wise Batching

A single outside Swiggy partner collects multiple student orders from popular partner restaurants in Coimbatore and brings them straight to the Main Campus Gate.

03

Gate-to-Hostel Relay

Zero unauthorized outside vehicles inside campus. Our verified internal Campus Riders pick up batched bags at the Main Gate and complete the last-mile hostel drop.

Swiggy Food for Thought Hackathon

Swiggy Campus Connect — Concept Pitch

01 / The Campus Problem

Small breaks. Endless queues.

At residential campuses like Amrita Ettimadai, students lose short breaks walking to MBA, IT, or MC canteens, facing long queues and unpredictable item stock with no hostel-gate delivery.

02 / The Proposed Solution

One campus. One food feed.

A unified Swiggy storefront combining all 3 canteens with canteen-owned live stock toggles, backed by a centrally managed fixed pool of verified on-campus riders.

03 / Three Core Service Rules
1. Unified Menu

Search once and compare dishes & prices across MBA, IT, and MC canteens.

2. Shared Rider Pool

5 fixed campus riders dynamically assigned across all canteens by Admin Ops.

3. Smart Peak Slots

Instant delivery off-peak; scheduled 15-min slots during lunch & evening rush.

04 / Stakeholder Value Exchange
Students

Zero queues, live stock visibility & hostel-gate drop.

Swiggy

High-frequency Gen-Z habit loop & micro-logistics pilot.

Canteens

Digital order queue & instant stock control.

College

Verified riders, designated gates & campus safety.

05 / 4-Phase Pilot Roadmap
Phase 1: Discovery

Student survey validation & college permissions.

Phase 2: Pilot

Launch 3 canteens + 5 riders across hostel gates.

Phase 3: Tune

Optimize delivery SLA, slot caps & unit economics.

Phase 4: Scale

Add outside batch delivery & expand to other campuses.
